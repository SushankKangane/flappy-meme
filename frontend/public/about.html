About Flappy Meme

Flappy Meme is a free, browser-based game inspired by the classic Flappy Bird. What makes it unique is the ability to fully customize your gaming experience with your own meme images and sounds.

What is Flappy Meme?

Flappy Meme is an online casual game where you control a character by tapping or clicking to make it fly through gaps between obstacles. The game gets progressively harder as the speed increases over time. Your goal is to achieve the highest score possible and share it with friends.

Key Features

  • Custom Player Images – Upload any image to use as your in-game character. Use your favorite meme, a photo of your pet, or any image you like.
  • Custom Obstacle Images – Replace the default obstacle images with your own to personalize the game world.
  • Custom Hit Sounds – Upload your own audio file that plays when you hit an obstacle.
  • Score Sharing – Easily share your high scores on social media or copy them to your clipboard.
  • Mobile Friendly – Optimized for both desktop and mobile devices with responsive controls and layout.
  • Progressive Difficulty – The game speeds up over time, challenging you to beat your best score.

How to Play

Playing Flappy Meme is simple. Visit the homepage, optionally upload custom images and sounds, then click "Start Game." Tap the screen, click your mouse, or press the Space bar to make your character jump. Navigate through the gaps between pipes to score points. The game ends when you hit a pipe or the ground.

Who Made This?

Flappy Meme was created by a small team of game and meme enthusiasts who wanted to bring a fun, customizable twist to the classic Flappy Bird concept. We believe gaming should be fun, accessible, and personal.
