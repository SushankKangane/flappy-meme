Frequently Asked Questions

Is Flappy Meme free to play?

Yes, Flappy Meme is completely free. You can play directly in your browser without downloading or installing anything.

Are my uploaded images stored anywhere?

No. All images and audio files you upload are processed entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to our servers. Your data stays on your device.

Can I play on mobile?

Absolutely. Flappy Meme is fully optimized for mobile devices. Just open the website in your mobile browser and tap to play.

How do I report a bug?

Send us an email at flappymeme2@gmail.com with a description of the issue and the device/browser you are using. We will look into it as soon as possible.
